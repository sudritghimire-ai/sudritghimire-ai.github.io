Sudrit Ghimire

Undergraduate researcher · San Marcos, Texas

I am a computer science undergraduate at Texas State University, with a minor in Data Analytics. Originally from Nepal, I work at the intersection of formal verification, computer systems, and computational number theory.

My research explores how mathematical reasoning and verified implementations can make computing systems more trustworthy. I work on order-sensitive streaming evidence for Linux telemetry and on constructing and verifying widely digitally delicate primes.

aui25@txstate.edu/Selected work ↓

Research interests

  • Formal verification & systems

    Verified Rust implementations, Verus proofs, and the connection between mathematical specifications and systems code.

  • Streaming evidence & telemetry

    Compact, order-sensitive fingerprints for event streams, Linux telemetry, and eBPF-based collection.

  • Computational number theory

    Digitally delicate primes, modular covering systems, and verified computational constructions.
